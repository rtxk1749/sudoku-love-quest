Hidden within the completed Sudoku is a 6-digit code.

Once you've solved the puzzle, read the marked cells in this order:

♥ → ★ → ☀ → ✿ → ♫ → ◆
Use 0 for ♥ and ◆.

For ★, ☀, ✿ and ♫, use the number inside their marked Sudoku cell.
